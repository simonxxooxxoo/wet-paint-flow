Paint Flow. A public-domain artwork and a digital photograph of that artwork
are separate rights questions, so both are recorded below.

## Rights-policy summary

- [The Met Open Access FAQ](https://www.metmuseum.org/pt/policies/frequently-asked-questions-image-and-data-resources)
  permits designated OA images for commercial and noncommercial use without
  permission or fee, permits manipulation, and does not require attribution.
- [RISD Museum](https://risdmuseum.org/image-request-form) provides its digital
  images of public-domain collection materials for any purpose; it requests a
  courtesy credit where possible.
- [Minneapolis Institute of Art](https://new.artsmia.org/copyright-and-image-access)
  says images identified with the Public Domain Mark are free of known
  copyright restrictions and may be copied, modified, distributed, and used
  commercially without permission.
- Wikimedia Commons' PD-Art notices treat faithful reproductions of
  two-dimensional public-domain works as public domain in the United States.
  Each relevant file page also warns that reuse can be restricted in other
  jurisdictions. Downstream distributors should assess their target country.

The following official-museum website images were deliberately not used:

- National Gallery images are [CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/documentation/ngacuk/licences),
  which does not fit transformed, commercially reusable open-source assets.
- [MoMA's API terms](https://api.moma.org/Home/Terms) put its dataset under
  CC0 but explicitly exclude images and direct image licensing elsewhere.
- The [Pushkin Museum website terms](https://www.pushkinmuseum.art/usage_policy/index.php?lang=en)
  limit ordinary website-image reuse to noncommercial educational, cultural,
  and scientific uses, generally no more than 800 px on the long edge with a
  source link; other reproduction requires agreement.
- The [Van Gogh Museum's collection-image conditions](https://www.vangoghmuseum.nl/assets/05aed642-6e2a-46e6-bb93-3a6826d63e91/Conditions-for-Use-of-Collection-Images-Van-Gogh-Museum-2022?c=1ae93a4fe2d6eb2875672ed16d59058741397476be6cd1c15ca16cc5e924d786)
  allow limited noncommercial web and educational use at restricted sizes;
  commercial use requires a separate request. The project instead uses the
  Commons PD-Art file listed above.

WikiArt was used only as a possible discovery lead. Availability on WikiArt is
not treated as evidence that its digital file may be redistributed.
